Risk Mitigation

RISK PLAN

🏔️ Altitude Sickness HIGH

  • Day 1 swing large: 500→5922→3850, highest risk on first day
  • Rhodiola mandatory 3 days pre-departure, everyone
  • Each car: 2 oxygen + 4 glucose
  • Bipenggou don't enter deep Niuxingou · Stay ≤2h
  • Symptoms → immediately descend to Lixian (1888m)
  • Day 2 AM check · Severe cases skip Maobiliang

🌧️ Weather HIGH

  • Late Sept western Sichuan rainy · Check forecast 7 days before
  • Rainy day: prioritize in-car + camp · Less outdoor
  • Potou→Bipenggou landslide risk after rain
  • Dengshenggou + Balangshan pass high rain-fog probability
  • Raincoat + lens rain cover ×5 (1 per car)

🚗 Road Conditions MED

  • Lixiao Road (Potou→Bipenggou→Liangtaigou→Daerpu) sharp turns + single-lane yield sections
  • Dengshenggou to Balangshan pass rockfall risk
  • Offline maps per car · Mark signal-loss zones
  • Fleet length ≤400m · Car distance ≥80m
  • Drivers must study route pre-departure · Unfamiliar drivers excluded

📵 Communication MED

  • Potou→Bipenggou→Liangtaigou + Dengshenggou no cell signal
  • Walkie-talkie main channel all-fleet · Backup channel lead only
  • Pre-mark no-signal zones · Agree emergency code
  • Each car has emergency contact sticker inside
  • Rescue / medical / 4S phone numbers pre-saved in lead coordinator phone

🎬 Filming Continuity MED

  • Camera car backup (photo assistant in Car D)
  • Main camera 8+8 batteries · High altitude drains fast
  • Every night mandatory backup to 2 hard drives
  • Pre-scout drone launch/land points · Watch plateau wind
  • Camera car reserves +20min buffer per node

👥 Personnel Fatigue MED

  • 4 veteran shots all on Day 2 · High energy cost
  • Day 2 lunch mandatory 1h rest · Veterans may opt out
  • New staff pair with veterans · Veterans can relax
  • No evening activity · Reserve 30min personal time
  • Fever / cold / severe fatigue → no high altitude
